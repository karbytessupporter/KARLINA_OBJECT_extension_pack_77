At the time of this writing it is approximately 9:05PM Pacific Standard Time on 10_SEPTEMBER_2026. The purpose of this note is to redress some of what I said in earlier notes which were published within the past 72 hours...

Firstly, I would like to formally redact what I said about wanting to inflict violence on any person. I would rather exhaust non-violent and minimally harmful solutions before resorting to violence as my response to any provocation. Also, if I am forced into a situation where it seems that either me or the other party must be eliminated or otherwise be made to take a loss, I propose that I would personally volunteer to sacrifice myself on the other party's behalf in the hopes that doing so would end the fight and would set a good example for other people of what it means to be morally decent.

Secondly, I would like to formally redact what I said about women being inferior to men in any way (or vice versa). My position now is that neither gender is smarter, more good-looking, more professional, more intelligent, or more ethical than the other. It is neither fair nor scientifically accurate to reduce complex human beings down to overly simplistic and biased stereotypes. Also, I no longer think that women need to dress or otherwise present themselves in a way which diminishes their femininity in order for them to come across to me as professionally competent or worthy of my respect.
