## How the hidden information is extracted

JPEGs are structured binary files rather than undifferentiated blocks of pixels. They contain marker segments with particular purposes:

* `APP0` commonly holds JFIF information such as format version and pixel-density conventions.

* `APP1` commonly holds EXIF or XMP metadata.

* `APP2` can hold an ICC color profile.

* `APP13` commonly holds IPTC information.

* `SOF0` contains fundamental image properties such as width, height, component count, sampling factors, and bit depth.

* Quantization and Huffman-table segments describe how the pixels were compressed.

* A `COM` segment can contain a textual JPEG comment.
