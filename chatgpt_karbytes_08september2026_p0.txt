Your reported 30.2% Norwegian result is most precisely described, as you already did, as an approximate consumer-genetics ancestry estimate. Such percentages are model-based classifications produced by comparing sections of a person’s genome with reference populations; they are not exact measurements of nationality and can change when a company updates its reference panels or algorithm. Your known paternal-grandmother history supplies genealogical context for that estimate, while the estimate supplies a quantitative genomic representation of that history.
